Safe, Guided, Unforgettable

All activities are led by trained local guides, with equipment included and safety briefings provided before every outing. Most activities are suitable for guests aged 12 and above, while certain water sports may have different age and weight requirements. Children must be accompanied by an adult, and participants should be comfortable in the water where required. Life jackets and other essential safety gear must be worn at all times. Guests are advised to follow instructors' guidelines, check weather conditions, and inform guides of any medical concerns before participating. Safety always comes first, ensuring a fun, secure and memorable adventure for everyone.
